## Iteration 2 — Cardiovascular + Hormonal
- **Search queries:**
  - "exercise timing cardiovascular performance heart rate blood pressure morning evening RCT systematic review"
  - "exercise timing cortisol testosterone diurnal variation hormonal response morning evening"
- **Papers found (5 new + 1 reclassified):**
  1. Brito et al. (2019) — Evening training reduced BP in hypertensive men (Med Sci Sports Exerc)
  2. Shen et al. (2025) — Evening exercise improved vascular function; morning better for metabolic (Sci Rep)
  3. Hayes et al. (2010) — Review: evening T/C ratio more favorable (Chronobiol Int)
  4. Bird & Tarpenning (2004) — Evening exercise: lower cortisol, better T/C ratio (Chronobiol Int)
  5. Sedliak et al. (2007) — Morning training reduced resting cortisol (Chronobiol Int)
  6. Sevilla-Lorente et al. (2023) — Reclassified: meta-analysis showing no timing difference for CVD risk
